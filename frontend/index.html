Researcher setup

Provisional shortened schedule: the first trial starts after one onset interval. With the defaults, onsets are at 40, 80, 120, 160, and 200 seconds; the session ends at 240 seconds unless extinction extends the final episode. Original study settings: 30-second windows, 60-second onset intervals, usually 9 trials.

“Sit!” is available alongside the selected caregiver response throughout the session. Penny starts each trial standing and remains seated after “Sit!” until the next trial. Sitting never stops barking. There are no probability changes or black screens.

Extinction overlap rule for this prototype: if barking extends past a scheduled onset, that onset is skipped and logged. The ongoing episode and posture continue. No extra trial is added. This rule is an implementation choice, not a procedure specified in the article.
